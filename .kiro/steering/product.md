# AssetQL Product Overview

AssetQL is an AI-powered creative asset production automation platform that transforms bulk digital asset creation into a scalable production pipeline.

## Core Problem

Creative teams struggle with generating hundreds of style-consistent, production-ready assets. Existing AI tools generate individual images but lack workflow automation, style consistency enforcement, and asset lifecycle management.

## Solution

AssetQL is a style-locked bulk asset automation system that:
- Generates 100-500+ style-consistent images from CSV input
- Enforces brand consistency through AI-powered style embeddings
- Automates tagging, categorization, and thumbnail generation
- Provides platform-specific exports (Unity, CMS, e-commerce, social media)
- Manages complete asset lifecycle from generation to distribution

## Target Users

- Game developers (character variations, item assets, textures)
- Marketing agencies (campaign assets across channels)
- Social media managers (daily content variations)
- E-commerce teams (product lifestyle images)
- Digital content teams (multi-platform content libraries)

## Key Differentiators

1. Production-scale focus (100-500+ asset batches vs. individual images)
2. Style consistency engine with AI-powered deviation scoring
3. End-to-end workflow automation (upload → generate → tag → deliver)
4. AI-powered asset intelligence (auto-tagging, categorization, thumbnails)
5. Cost-optimized AI models (Amazon Nova Lite, Stable Image Core)
6. Serverless architecture optimized for batch economics

## Success Metrics

- 70-90% reduction in manual generation time
- 85%+ style consistency across batches
- <$0.50 cost per asset (50x cheaper AI models)
- 95%+ batch completion rate
- 100 assets generated in <30 minutes
